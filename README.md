# France_Italy_far_right_MCA
A research project that our group did for the course of "Enquete Sociologique" during the third year of bachelor's of sociology at Sorbonne University. The title of the project is "We Just Want Order - Political and Social Imaginaries of Far-Right Voters in Italy and France", unfortunately, the text is written in French, but I've also added a research project of this project in english just in case. 

Data: European Social Survey wave 11

Contents: Code_for_inter.R - the main code, there is also a text of the projects, visualisations and the english version of the research project

Personal contribution: I wrote all of the code, made all of the visualisations and wrote: methodology part, supplementary variables analysis, limites and future of the research. Also I contributed to inventing the hypotheses and the MCA result part. 

Main findings: This study finds that opposition to immigration constitutes a common ideological foundation among far-right electorates in both France and Italy, structuring the primary dimension of the MCA and accounting for approximately 55% of corrected variance in both countries. However, the two national contexts diverge significantly on a second dimension related to religiosity and traditionalism. In Italy, religious and traditional values form a unified conservative register, closely associated with anti-immigration attitudes and consistent across party, age, and occupational groups. In France, this dimension is driven primarily by religiosity alone, with traditionalism playing a secondary role, and the far-right electorate appears more internally fragmented — differentiated by party affiliation and socioprofessional category. These results suggest that while a transnational ideological core exists around immigration, far-right political imaginaries remain anchored in distinct national cultural and historical contexts.
